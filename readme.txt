Customer delivery date and time slot selection at WooCommerce checkout, with carrier and schedule management.

== Description ==

Jezpress WooCommerce Delivery Dates lets your customers select a delivery date and time slot at checkout. Delivery schedules and carriers are managed in the WordPress admin under WooCommerce > Delivery Dates.

**Features:**
* Customer date and time slot selection on the classic WooCommerce checkout
* Carrier management — add, edit, and order multiple delivery carriers
* Schedule management — add individual date/time slots or bulk-generate recurring weekly schedules
* Max orders per slot with real-time booking count tracking
* Delivery date shown in the admin order page, customer order detail page, and all WooCommerce emails
* Delivery Date column in the WooCommerce orders list
* Configurable settings: minimum advance booking, maximum future days, required/optional, checkout label, carrier visibility
* JezPress license and auto-update integration

**Note:** WooCommerce Blocks checkout is not supported in v1.0. Requires the classic checkout shortcode.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the `jezpress-woo-delivery-dates` folder to `/wp-content/plugins/`.
2. Activate the plugin in *Plugins > Installed Plugins*.
3. Activate your license key under *WooCommerce > Delivery Dates > License*.
4. Go to *WooCommerce > Delivery Dates > Carriers* to add at least one carrier.
5. Go to *WooCommerce > Delivery Dates > Schedules* to add delivery slots.
6. Configure general options under *WooCommerce > Delivery Dates > General Settings*.
